ClipFarm privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who uses ClipFarm

ClipFarm is a private tool used only by its owner, on their own computer, for their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data ClipFarm accesses

When the owner connects a channel, ClipFarm asks Google for permission to:

  • upload and schedule videos on that channel,
  • view the channel's basic details (its name and ID), and
  • view the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (such as views and subscribers).

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to schedule the owner's uploads and to show the owner their own channel statistics.
  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. ClipFarm has no server and sends this data nowhere except to Google's own APIs.
  • The data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone.
  • The data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

ClipFarm's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipFarm uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting a channel folder's .secrets folder removes its saved sign-in from the computer.
